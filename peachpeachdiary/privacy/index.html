In short: Peach Peach Diary does not collect any personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Peach Peach Diary does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

  • No account or login is required.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, location, or any other personally identifiable information.
  • Game progress and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to external servers.

2. Third-Party Services

Peach Peach Diary does not use any third-party services such as advertising SDKs, analytics tools, or social logins. The app works fully offline and does not require an internet connection.

3. Children's Privacy

Since Peach Peach Diary does not collect any personal information, no data is collected from any users, including children.

4. Data Deletion

All locally stored data, such as game progress, is removed when you delete the app.

5. Changes to This Policy

If a future update changes how the app handles data — for example, by adding ads or analytics — this policy will be updated and posted on this page.
